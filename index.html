Get to Know the Person Behind the Words.

Hi, Welcome to My Little Corner of the Web!

I'm a Principal Technical Writer with 15+ years of experience transforming complex software and technology concepts into clear, useful, and user-focused content.

My experience spans software, hardware, and mobile application documentation, including online help, release notes, user guides, training materials, reference documentation, and documentation modernization. I lead documentation initiatives, collaborate with cross-functional teams, and continuously look for better ways to plan, create, maintain, and deliver technical content.

I'm experienced in DITA XML, Markdown, GitHub, Azure, content migration, structured authoring, documentation workflows, and content quality management. I also enjoy bridging the gap between technical teams and users by turning complex information into documentation that is easier to understand and use.

As the content landscape evolves, I'm actively exploring Generative AI, AI-assisted content development, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), API documentation, and AI-driven content workflows. I have applied AI to real-world documentation processes, including developing a Release Notes GenAI Tracking Tool to support AI adoption and improve workflow efficiency within a documentation team.

“People make the decisions; AI does the labor.”

I believe AI should augment human expertise—not replace human judgment. People bring context, creativity, critical thinking, and accountability; AI can help handle repetitive work, accelerate workflows, and give people more time to focus on higher-value tasks.

My focus is not simply on writing documentation—it's on improving how technical content is created, managed, and experienced.

My commitment to innovation and continuous improvement was recognized with 3rd Place in Deltek's Idea Fest Innovation 2021 competition.

I'm always interested in learning, solving documentation challenges, exploring emerging technologies, and connecting with professionals who value clear communication, innovation, and continuous improvement.
